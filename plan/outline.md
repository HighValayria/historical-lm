## Evidence Rules

- Record source type, date accessed, direct support versus inference, and uncertainty for important claims.
- Prefer primary papers, official project pages, original datasets, institutional libraries, archival catalogues, and official documentation.
- Explicitly track archival survival bias, literacy and class bias, regional bias, author and publisher bias, OCR bias, modern editorial intervention, and digitization bias.
