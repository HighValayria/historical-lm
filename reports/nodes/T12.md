# T12 Node Report: Digital-Humanities Concept Map

## Objective

Define concepts and operational indicators for historical knowledge boundaries, historical ignorance, archival bias, anachronism, presentism, provenance uncertainty, and source misattribution.

## Dependencies

T00.

## Questions Resolved

- Historical knowledge boundary is not merely a model training cutoff. It must include what a source ecology or historical actor could plausibly know, what survives, and what can be cited.
- Anachronism must be separated into factual, lexical, causal, value, source-attribution, and provenance dimensions.
- Historically appropriate ignorance should be an explicit positive behavior, not a generic model failure.
- A benchmark needs fields for date, source family, source population, audience, provenance confidence, and answer policy.

## Inputs

- T12 subagent concept map.
- Historiographic, archival-studies, digital-humanities, temporal NLP, and attribution/NLG references recorded in `evidence/concept_map.md`.
- T10 and T11 findings about method and source feasibility.

## Planned Actions

- Define working concepts.
- Convert concepts into benchmark indicators.
- Identify conceptual risks.

## Actual Outcome

The concept map now includes definitions, operational signals, risks, and a draft benchmark label schema. It explicitly prevents three common mistakes:

1. Treating the present-day truth of a claim as sufficient for a historical answer.
2. Treating digitized accessible archives as representative of all late-Qing people.
3. Treating abstention or uncertainty as failure when the historical evidence is unavailable, post-cutoff, or not accessible to the target actor.

## Changed Assumptions

- Candidate topics must specify source population and target actor/source ecology.
- Evaluation should reward caveated, source-grounded answers and historically appropriate refusal.
- Prompt design must distinguish actor-level reconstruction from present-day analysis.

## Risks

- Some conceptual terms are project-specific operationalizations and need pilot annotation to become stable.
- The benchmark could over-penalize legitimate present-day analysis unless prompts specify the requested standpoint.
