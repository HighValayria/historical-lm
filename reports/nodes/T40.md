# T40 Node Report: Design Anachronism Benchmark

## Objective

Design a benchmark specification for historically bounded late-Qing public-health answering.

## Dependencies

## Inputs

- T12 concept map.
- T21 recommended direction.
- T30/T31 access and OCR metadata probes.

## Actual Outcome

Created:

- `benchmarks/pilot/benchmark_spec.md`
- `benchmarks/pilot/schema.json`

The benchmark design supports post-cutoff controls, metadata/provenance checks, source-grounded factual items, lexical/causal/value anachronism, source misattribution, and historical ignorance.

## Key Design Decisions

- Source-grounded factual, lexical, causal, and value-anachronism items require verified historical passages.
- Metadata-only and post-cutoff control items may be used before passage extraction, but must be clearly labelled.
- `needs_source_excerpt` placeholders are allowed for design but must not be scored.
- Abstention and caveated answers are explicit positive behaviors.

## Changed Assumptions

- T41 can create a pilot seed dataset without pretending that all items are source-grounded.
- The first engineering probe can validate schema and retrieval mechanics even before full OCR extraction.
