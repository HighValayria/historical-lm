# T31 Node Report: Probe OCR and Metadata Quality

## Objective

Probe OCR/text and metadata quality signals for T30-reachable source candidates.

## Dependencies

T30.

## Inputs

- `probes/data_access/access_probe_results.json`
- `probes/ocr/probe_text_signals.ps1`

## Actions

- Created and ran a metadata/link-signal probe.
- Checked four reachable source pages for PDF, OCR, full-text, download, IIIF/manifest, rights, date, and title signals.
- Saved results in `probes/ocr/ocr_metadata_probe_results.json`.
- Saved a human-readable summary in `probes/ocr/ocr_metadata_probe_summary.md`.
- Did not save page bodies, download PDFs, or perform bulk extraction.

## Actual Outcome

- `SRC-CUSTOMS-WELLCOME`: high follow-up feasibility; PDF/download/IIIF signals detected.
- `SRC-CMMJ-CRL`: high follow-up feasibility; download and IIIF signals detected.
- `SRC-SMC-BNASIE`: medium follow-up feasibility; direct PDF and online reader links detected.
- `SRC-CUSTOMS-AS`: low feasibility from landing page alone; no direct text/download links detected by simple regex.

## Key Findings

- Metadata is adequate on all four pages.
- Explicit OCR signals were not found on any page by keyword search.
- A first benchmark should rely on curated excerpts and metadata rather than assume a clean OCR corpus.

## Changed Assumptions

- T41 benchmark items should be hand-curated from a small number of source excerpts.
- T51 engineering should prototype retrieval over curated snippets and citation metadata, not over a large extracted corpus.

## Failures and Negative Findings

- No page explicitly exposed OCR signals through the simple keyword probe.
- Academia Sinica's landing page did not expose direct candidate PDF/full-text links through the simple regex probe.

## Follow-up Tasks

- T41: build a pilot benchmark with curated excerpts and citation metadata.
- T51: implement a minimal retrieval/evaluation prototype that works without bulk OCR.

## Completion Criteria Check
